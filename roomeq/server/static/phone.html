One-time setup: trust RoomEQ on this phone

Safari only allows the microphone over HTTPS. RoomEQ made its own certificate. Install it once:

  1. Download the RoomEQ certificate, then tap Allow.
  2. Open Settings → General → VPN & Device Management, tap RoomEQ Local CA, then Install.
  3. Open Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings and switch on RoomEQ Local CA.
  4. Then open the secure page.
